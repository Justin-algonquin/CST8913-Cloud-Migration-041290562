# Lab 2: Levi Strauss & Co. Cloud Migration Case Study

## 1. Migration Motivators

Levi Strauss & Co. migrated to the cloud because its data centers were expensive to maintain. The company also needed more speed and agility to support its business operations. Another important reason was to improve the performance and resilience of its systems.
## 2. Infrastructure Discovery Questions

- **Applications:** Which applications do you want to migrate to the cloud?
- **Servers:** What are the specifications and configurations of the existing servers?
- **Data:** How much data does the company currently have to migrate?
- **Networking:** What is the current network configuration, and what network requirements must be maintained in the cloud?
- **Security:** What security measures are currently used to protect the company's systems and data?
- **Dependencies:** What dependencies exist between the current applications, databases, and other systems?
- **Business Requirements:** What do you want to improve in your business operations, and what challenges are you experiencing with the current systems?

## 4. Migration Approach

I would choose the refactor approach because Levi Strauss & Co. wanted to modernize its existing technology environment, not only move it to the cloud. Refactoring would allow the company to redesign applications to better use cloud services and improve performance, scalability, and resilience.
## 5. High-Level Migration Schedule

| Phase | Activity |
|---|---|
| 1. Assessment and Discovery | Analyze existing applications, servers, data, network, security, and dependencies. |
| 2. Requirements and Planning | Identify business and technical requirements, costs, and prepare the migration plan. |
| 3. Azure Environment Preparation | Configure networking, security, storage, and access in Azure. |
| 4. Migration and Deployment | Migrate and deploy applications and data to Azure. |
| 5. Testing and Validation | Test the migrated systems and verify performance and security. |
| 6. Go-live and Monitoring | Put the systems into production and monitor performance and reliability. |
## 6. Main Decision Criteria

**Business criteria:**
- Cost
- Business benefits
- Impact on business operations
- Agility and scalability

**Technical criteria:**
- Availability and reliability
- Security
- Performance
- Compatibility with Azure
